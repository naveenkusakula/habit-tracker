# Privacy Policy — Streaks

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Streaks is a daily habit tracker designed to keep your information on your
device. This policy is short because there is very little to explain.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Streaks does not collect, store remotely, sell, or share any
personal data.

- The app declares **no permissions** — it cannot access the internet,
  your location, contacts, photos, or any other data on your device.
- Your habits and streaks are stored only in the app's private storage on
  your device. They are never transmitted anywhere, and cloud backup and
  device-transfer of app data are disabled.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
  SDKs or trackers in the app.

## What happens to your data

Your habit data exists only on your device, under your control. Deleting a
habit removes it from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its
data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes this policy, the updated policy will be
published with that version before it takes effect.
